ABC Health Prior Authorization Rules

MRI Lumbar Spine:

Prior authorization is required for MRI Lumbar Spine.

Clinical documentation should include:
- The patient's diagnosis.
- Duration and severity of symptoms.
- Relevant conservative treatment history.
- Clinical reason for requesting the MRI.

The request should include the patient's information, provider information, procedure information, diagnosis, and supporting clinical documentation.

Requests with missing required clinical documentation may be delayed or denied.
